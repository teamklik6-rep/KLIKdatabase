## Стиль 2 — Візантійська монограма: чорне на пергаменті

Хрест-христограма в ритмі IC XC NIKA, але тільки слово бренду: FA | R зверху, SE | ER знизу, титла, крапки на кінцях рамен і у внутрішніх кутах.

```
The attached image is a style reference only: take its Byzantine christogram structure and spirit, the flared cross, rounded uncial lettering, titla and solid black ink; do not copy its letters.
Flat vector Byzantine christogram-style logo for "FARSEER", near-black ink on a warm off-white parchment background, the cross filling the centre of the frame. An equal-armed cross whose four arms are slightly concave along their length and flare at the ends into cupped, swallowtail-like terminals; a small round dot floats just beyond the end of each arm, and four tiny dots sit in the inner angles where the arms meet. The four quadrants between the arms carry only the letters of the brand name in rounded medieval uncial capitals, read row by row like an icon inscription: "FA" in the upper left and "R" in the upper right, "SE" in the lower left and "ER" in the lower right. Every letter group sits close to the vertical arm with the same small gap, so the top row reads FAR and the bottom row reads SEER. A small wavy titlo stroke floats above "FA" and above "R". Crisp clean vector edges, balanced composition, strictly two colours: near-black ink is the only colour on the parchment, and these brand letters are the only lettering in the image. Front-on flat logo presentation with no mockup, no perspective, no shadow and no gradient.
```

In the centre of the seal, an archaic eye: an almond outline drawn with one confident even stroke, a solid black round iris with a small parchment-coloured pupil in its middle, three short straight rays fanning out above the eye and three below it.
